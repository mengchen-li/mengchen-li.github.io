  Designed and built a working web prototype (React and the Claude API) in which learners commit to a prediction before any answer appears, then investigate, represent, reflect on, and transfer a concept through a six-phase cycle. A brief intake profile tunes the AI tutor's support style to each learner. The accompanying portfolio traces three rounds of design-based iteration and grounds each design decision in learning sciences research, from productive failure to multiple representations.
