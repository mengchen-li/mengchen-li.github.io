description: >-
  Built an image classifier with Teachable Machine that tells whether a study seat is empty, held by belongings, or occupied, a problem I run into every week in my building's study room, which has no booking system. When early tests showed unstable confidence for empty seats, I rebalanced the training data and retrained; the final model labeled every held-out photo correctly, including those from a room it had never seen. The write-up also designs privacy and error-reporting safeguards for a real deployment.
