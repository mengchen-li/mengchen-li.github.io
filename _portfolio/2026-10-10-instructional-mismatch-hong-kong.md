description: >-
  Examines why lessons in Hong Kong secondary classrooms can be pitched above or below what a class already knows, in a system where banded schools share one curriculum and one public examination, and where teachers increasingly draft plans with generative AI. Using cognitive load theory and constructivism, the analysis explains how mismatch accumulates for students and constrains teachers, then marks where theories of individual learning stop explaining a classroom-level problem.
