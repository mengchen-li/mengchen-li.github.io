  A comparative review of three teacher policies, each targeting a different stage of the career cycle: teacher residencies for preparation, the IMPACT evaluation system in Washington, D.C., for development, and Tennessee's Priority School Retention Bonus for retention. For each, the paper weighs the evidence on outcomes against the conditions needed for replication, then draws cross-policy lessons on problem focus, incentive size, and policy stability.
