title: "Feature Engineering for Off-Task Detection from Action-Level Tutor Logs"
date: 2026-10-15
category: Educational Data Mining
description: >-
  Returned to the raw action log behind my first off-task detector and engineered 24 features that clip-level averages cannot express, including action counts, longest pauses, skill coverage, and Bayesian Knowledge Tracing estimates. Under the same student-grouped nested cross-validation, the new detector raised held-out Kappa from 0.31 to 0.38 and AUC from 0.75 to 0.88, outperforming the old-feature detector in every repeat. Timing shape and timing contrasts carried most of the signal.
