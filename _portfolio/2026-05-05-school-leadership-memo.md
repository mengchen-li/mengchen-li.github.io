  A consulting memo for the incoming principal of a young high school facing repeated safety incidents, stalled achievement, an upcoming accreditation review, and a divided leadership team. It sequences three priorities: using the safety decision to establish a new decision-making culture, matching leadership style to each team member's readiness, and building a governance council that makes honest professional dialogue routine. The recommendations draw on situational, culturally responsive, and distributed leadership research.
