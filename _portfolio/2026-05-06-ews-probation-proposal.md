title: "The Effect of Early Warning System Probation on Undergraduate Student Success"
date: 2026-05-06
category: Research Proposal
description: >-
  A causal research design for evaluating early warning systems in higher education. A regression discontinuity at the 2.0 GPA probation cutoff estimates effects on next-term GPA, credit completion, and one-year retention; an instrumental variables approach then separates the effect of being flagged from the effect of actually meeting an advisor. The design also tests whether effects differ by race/ethnicity and gender, responding to equity concerns about algorithmic prediction in education.
