title: "Can a Small Language Model Leak Private Chat Data? A Prompt-Testing Study"
date: 2026-10-03
category: AI Literacy Experiment
description: >-
  Trained a tiny language model on a fictional chat log in which invented students share IDs, emails, and passwords, then probed whether it would hand one user's private details to another. Across 80 controlled prompts, the model leaked a planted student ID in 48, often to entirely different names, and privacy requests written into the data did nothing to stop it; a model trained on scrubbed data leaked nothing. The study closes with a classroom activity design for introductory high school computer science.
